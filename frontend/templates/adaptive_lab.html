How Goal B calculates its threshold

Step 1: Calculate the interval Z-score using the learned mean and standard deviation.

Step 2: Divide observed jitter by reference jitter to obtain the jitter ratio.

Step 3: Adjust the threshold using:

T = 3 + min(0.5, 0.5 x max(0, jitter_ratio - 1))

Step 4: Report an anomaly when the Z-score exceeds the calculated threshold.

Research limitations

This adaptive method is a proposed heuristic. Its synthetic experiments showed fewer false positives but also some additional missed attacks. Different decisions alone do not establish whether an observation is truly benign or malicious.
